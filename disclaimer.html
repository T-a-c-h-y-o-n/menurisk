Disclaimer

Menu Risk is a recall monitoring and matching tool. It is not a food safety authority and not a certification body.

What a match means

Results are potential matches, produced by text-based comparison of your ingredient list against public US recall data. A match does not confirm that any product you hold is affected, and it does not mean a product is unsafe.

What a match does not mean

  • It is not a determination, a verdict, or a certification.
  • It is not proof of compliance, and it is not proof of non-compliance.
  • It is not legal, regulatory, medical or compliance advice.
  • A result with no matches is not an all-clear: new recalls are published continuously.

What you should do

  1. Open the source link for the match and read the agency's recall notice.
  2. Verify the lot, code and date on the product you actually have.
  3. Confirm with your supplier before using or serving it.
  4. Keep your own food safety procedures and records — they remain necessary.

Coverage and sources

Coverage is US recalls only. Recall data comes from public US government sources, chiefly the FDA openFDA food enforcement dataset. USDA FSIS coverage is being added. Menu Risk is not affiliated with the FDA, the USDA, or any government agency, and it does not publish official alerts.
