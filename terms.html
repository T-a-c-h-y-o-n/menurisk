Terms

These terms cover the free menu scan. They are written to be read, not to be survived.

What the service does

You submit an ingredient list. We compare it, using deterministic text matching, against active US food recall records published by the FDA. We show you potential matches with a risk level, the reason the match was produced, a source link and the date the data was pulled.

What it does not do

  • It does not determine whether your product is safe, affected, or compliant.
  • It does not replace your food safety procedures, your supplier checks, or your HACCP plan.
  • It does not provide legal, regulatory, medical or compliance advice.
  • It does not cover recalls outside the United States.

Your responsibilities

  • Submit lists you have the right to submit, and keep them accurate.
  • Verify lot codes, dates and suppliers before acting on any match, and review every result before it changes what you serve.
  • Don't use the service to send bulk automated requests or to resell results as your own monitoring product.

Limits

The free scan covers up to 50 items, one scan per email address per month, and is rate limited per connection. Result links expire after 24 hours. Recall data is provided as published by the agency; if a source is temporarily unavailable we say so instead of reporting that nothing was found.

Availability and warranty

The service is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. We aim to keep it running but we don't promise uninterrupted availability, and we're not liable for decisions made from a potential match that you did not verify. If something goes wrong, tell us at info@ai2eo.com and we'll fix it or run the check manually.
