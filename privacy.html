Privacy

Plain language, limited to what this service actually does. If a statement about security isn't here, it's because we can't stand behind it yet.

What we store

  • The ingredient list you submit, so we can run your scan and, if you enable it later, keep checking it.
  • Your email address, so we can send your result link and answer you if you write to us.
  • The results of your scans: matched recalls, risk levels, the reasons, and the timestamps.
  • Basic operational logs (for example: a scan ran, an email was attempted) so we can debug and prove the check happened.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish your list, and we don't sell it.
  • We don't share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure needed to run the service (hosting and email delivery).
  • We don't send your list to language models. Matching is deterministic and runs on our own server.
  • We don't run advertising or tracking pixels on this site beyond aggregate page analytics.

How long we keep it

Your list and your scan records are kept until you ask us to delete them, because comparing a new scan against your history is the point of the service. Result links sent by email expire after 24 hours.

Deleting your data

Email info@ai2eo.com with the address you used and ask for deletion. We'll remove your list, your scan records and your email from the active database and confirm it back to you.

Recalls data

Recall information comes from public US government sources, chiefly the FDA openFDA food enforcement dataset. It is not your data and it is not personal data. Coverage is US recalls only.
